All scent personas

  • The First Light

    Zest on warm skin. A bright, close scent for the first hour of the day, noticed only by whoever leans in.

  • The Sunlit Signal

    Citrus peel, green leaves and a clean trail that walks into the room a step behind you.

  • The Bright Entrance

    Sparkling top notes with real staying power. You arrive and the whole room brightens a shade.

  • The Quiet Signature

    Clean woods, cool herbs and a whisper of iris. Sharp, composed and kept to your own desk.

  • The Sharp Edit

    Crisp aromatics over dry cedar. The scent of someone who answers emails in full sentences.

  • The Boardroom Pull

    Structured woods and cold spice with a trail that says the meeting starts when you sit down.

  • The Skin Secret

    Soft musk, warm petals and a trace of amber, made for the distance of a whisper.

  • The Slow Burn

    Rose, resin and something darker underneath. It lingers on a scarf long after you've left.

  • The After-Hours Magnet

    Oud, amber and velvet florals that own the room after dark. Heads turn and nobody can say why.

  • The Sweet Confidant

    Vanilla, cream and a pinch of spice worn close. Dessert you keep to yourself.

  • The Golden Hour Guest

    Juicy fruit, warm amber and a pop of fizz. You make any table feel like a party.

  • The Grand Entrance

    Rum, praline, saffron and leather. Loud in the best way and still there at the last song.

  • The Soft Landing

    Lavender, sandalwood and clean musk. A Sunday with nowhere to be, bottled.

  • The Cashmere Evening

    Creamy woods and powdery vanilla that warm a room like a lamp turned low.

  • The Clean Stride

    Cool water, bright citrus and a snap of ginger. Fresh from the first step to the last.

  • The Open Air

    Sea salt, green leaves and wild herbs that trail behind you like wind off the water.

  • The Still Point

    Sandalwood, tea and a thread of incense. Close, unhurried and completely your own.

  • The Temple Smoke

    Frankincense, myrrh and dry woods drifting through the room like the last minutes of a ritual.
